Being a father and husband I can completely relate to the notion of distraction when it comes to using the web. In the real world its a rare moment when I get to sit at a computer and provide it with 100% of my undivided attention. And in a world where computing is going to become more and more ubiquitous this will become more and more relevant to the way we design our user experiences.

Following [James May](http://www.feralabs.com/aboutus.html "Feralabs") and [Sabrina Page's](http://www.feralabs.com/aboutus.html) presentation at [UX Brighton](http://uxbrighton.org.uk/) I have certainly been trying to take the time to consider the entire context from which people approach and use the websites and experiences I design. And opening an eye to users in the wild really underlines the importance of super duper usability.
