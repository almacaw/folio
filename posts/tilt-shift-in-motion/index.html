Kieth Loutit - A collection of tilt shift movies

2009-06-30 — 1 min read

Filmed in Sydney, using the docks, the fabulous harbour and Mardi Gras celebrations as his canvas Kieth Loutit explores tilt shift in full motion giving both a child like and possibly God like perspective to the world we live in.

All good stuff, particularly the carnival, although it works wonderfully in 'The Bathtub' [Bathtub V](http://vimeo.com/5137183) from [Keith Loutit](http://vimeo.com/keithloutit) on [Vimeo](http://vimeo.com). It kind of made me want to go and shoot the Notting Hill Carnival this year.
